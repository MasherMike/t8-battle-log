What Wavu adds, which changes one of your notes:

- **A throw that lands on an armored move is unbreakable** (Wavu, Throw page: "A throw landing on an armored move, such as against a power crush or during a parry, becomes unbreakable"). The Giant Swing on your b+1+2 wasn't a missed break. It was free.
- **Muscle Armor** (db+1+2, pc6~59, ,2 cancel on frames 17~60): Wavu counterplay says "Use lows or throws to challenge it." Your guide says throw it or wait; lows are the other answer.
- **There's no safe catch-all break button.** Wavu: "any incorrect button press will lock you out of breaking it." Tomahawk breaks 1+2, Giant Swing 1, Tijuana Twister 2. On counter hit the window shrinks (20 frames to 14 in general; bluespark versions 18 to 7).
- **The tell between Tomahawk and Giant Swing is the approach, not the arms.** Wavu counterplay: "Running throws: usually require a 1+2 break, especially Shining Wizard (f,f,F+2+4)", and Wavu notes Giant Swing is "actually a 1 break" despite looking like a 1+2. Tomahawk comes off a dash; Giant Swing is a half-circle from where he stands.
