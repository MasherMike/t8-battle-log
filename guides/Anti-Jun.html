Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR) against her neutral pokes. Set by Tier 2, the TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview (community-written): "SSR (db3, df2, jabs, b4, db2, ...). At -1 you can SSR df1, at -3 (f4) you need SSL." The TekkenDocs sidestep chart (@Mezerdie, via sidestep.md) also says SSR. Tier 1 (Wavu's per-move notes) has nothing on her neutral pokes; its notes are all about later hits of strings, and those mostly go left. So the tally is not one-sided: see the evidence callout below.

Exceptions:
• String enders that whiff to the LEFT (Wavu, Tier 1): 2,1,4,1 (SWL from 1st block), df+3+4,1 (SWL from 1st block), 1+4,3,1 (SWL from 3rd block), ws1,4,1+2 (SSL / SWL from 2nd block). Mostly sidewalk left, not a single step.
• Two strings whiff to the RIGHT (Tier 1): f,F+3,3+4 (SSR from 1st block, +3 on block, the anti-strat expects it "to get spammed") and uf+4,3 (SSR / SWR).
• Genjitsu (GEN): don't step left. The anti-strat says "Against GEN: SSL block(safe), SWL(greedy)", but Wavu says GEN.1 (low) and H.GEN.1,1+2 (Heat Smash, low) both have "good left sidestep tracking", and GEN.4 is homing. Tier 1 wins: block GEN, don't SSL it.
• After her f+4 (+3~+5 on block), the anti-strat says SSL. f+4 itself is homing (Wavu): don't step it, duck it (it's a high).
• Homing (Wavu), don't step: 1,2,2, f+3,2, u+2 (all highs, +4 into IZU), f+4, GEN.4, df+3, f,F+3, IZU.f+1+2, MIA.1+2, SS.1+2, and her 1+3 / 2+4 throws.
• f+2 and df+2: Wavu says "Increased tracking when f2,1+2's input is buffered late".

So the habit is: SSR her pokes in neutral; once a string is running, SWL the 2,1,4, df+3+4 and ws1,4 enders; SSR the second hit of f,F+3; duck the homing highs; stand still and block against GEN.

Evidence tier: the neutral default comes from Tier 2 (TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview), because Tier 1 has no note on any of her neutral pokes. Tier 1 is Wavu's 10 whiff notes plus 2 tracking tags (TekkenDocs carries the same text): right-only uf+4,3, f,F+3,3+4 (2); left-only 2,1,4,1, df+3+4,1, 1+4,3,1, ws1,4,1+2 (4); either way 1+4,2, 1+4,3, b+3,2, hFC.3+4,3 (4); tracks SSL GEN.1, H.GEN.1,1+2 (2). The Tier 1 tally leans left 4–2, but every one of those is a late string hit, mostly a sidewalk, so it sets exceptions, not the default. Tier disagreement: the anti-strat says SSL vs GEN; Wavu says GEN.1 and H.GEN.1,1+2 track left steps, so the guide follows Tier 1 there. The sidestep chart (SSR) agrees with Tier 2. Nothing in this section is lab-tested.
Theory (Ledger): Against GEN, Wavu only says the two GEN lows track left, so a single SSR should beat GEN.1 and H.GEN.1,1+2. It still loses to GEN.4 (homing high, +4~+6) and GEN.2 (mid, power crush 8~15) is a guess either way. Test: after f,F+2~F (+1 into GEN), SSR against GEN.1, then against GEN.4.

Low Block Punishment↑ Return to Top

Reactability tag (Mike's reaction rule, using the low's fastest startup frame): Reactable 25f or slower · Mostly reactable 21–24f · Not reactable — guess or condition 20f or faster. Startups are from the Wavu movelist; a low with no stated startup gets no tag. For a string, the tag uses the startup Wavu lists for the low hit itself (its opener when the low comes first). Stance lows are tagged on their raw startup.
Her biggest low punish is in a stance: IZU.4 at −37. In neutral, 1+4,2,d+4 (−23~−22), FC.df+3 (−19) and ws1,4,2 (−16) launch. db+2, FC.df+2, GEN.1 and ws1,4,2 recover crouching (FC): punish those with a WS launcher. Most of her lows crush highs (Wavu cs frames on db+2, db+4, b+1, GEN.1, the FC lows), so a high jab whiffs into them.

Stance Counterplay↑ Return to Top

Jun has four stances: IZU (Izumo), GEN (Genjitsu), MIA (Miare) and SS (her sidestep stance). The plus entries and the jab arithmetic are in Stance Check. Can she block in them? No source says she can block in IZU, GEN or SS. For MIA the anti-strat says outright: "Against MIA: She can't block, and the cancel is slow." Wavu agrees that MIA "Stays in stance indefinitely" and cancels in 15f with DB.

General Counter Play↑ Return to Top

Practice priorities

1. Duck the IZU highs. 1,2,2, f+3,2 and u+2 are homing highs that put her +4 in IZU on block (Wavu). Duck them on a read, and launch the whiff.
