Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR) in neutral and at small minus frames. Set by Tier 1, the per-move step notes in Wavu (TekkenDocs repeats them): five of them favour the right side, one favours the left, three whiff either way. The TekkenDocs anti-strat page (community-written, Tier 2) agrees for her jab and kicks: "SSR for jab (tracks SSL!), df4, b4, 4, db4."

Exceptions:
• f+3 tracks your SSR (Wavu: "Has good right sidestep tracking"). It's a mid, −4, and goes into LIB +5 on hit. The anti-strat page says f3 can be stepped "both ways". The guide follows Tier 1: don't SSR it, block it.
• Her other mid pokes go LEFT (anti-strat): "SSL for left side moves; df1, df3(SWL). Exception is df2, seems easier to SSL." df+1 (i13~14, −3) is one of her most-used pokes, so the raw tally among her fastest pokes is close: jab and 4 go right, df+1 goes left.
• Once df+1 is blocked, switch to SSR: Wavu says df+1,4,1 "Whiffs vs SSR from 1st block". The anti-strat page calls it an "SSR block OS".
• The TekkenDocs sidestep chart (community chart by @Mezerdie, via sidestep.md) says SSL. It's the lowest tier, and both higher tiers point right for her jab and 4 strings, so the guide keeps SSR.
• Don't step LIB (anti-strat: "LIB is 'homing', don't step"). LIB.3+4 is homing in Wavu.
• Homing (Wavu), don't step: f+4 (mid), b+2 (high, +2), LIB.3+4 (high power crush), H.b+2,F, and her 1+3 / 2+4 throws.

So the habit is: SSR her jab, 4 and df+1-strings; block f+3 and df+1 itself; duck the homing highs (b+2, LIB.3+4); don't step at all once she's in LIB.

Theory (Ledger): The homing list splits cleanly by guard, so the SSR habit only needs one switch: duck the highs (b+2, +2; LIB.3+4, −2 and a power crush), block the mid (f+4, −8). H.b+2,F starts from b+2, a high, so the same duck should cover it. Test: duck b+2 on a read and check what you get on the whiff.

Low Block Punishment↑ Return to Top

Reactability tag (Mike's reaction rule, using the low's fastest startup frame): Reactable 25f or slower · Mostly reactable 21–24f · Not reactable — guess or condition 20f or faster. Startups are from the Wavu movelist; a low with no stated startup gets no tag. For a string, the tag is for the opening low, using Wavu's startup for it. Stance lows are tagged on their raw startup.
Her lows are fast and mostly punishable, but only d+1+2 (UFO Roll, i25~26) is slow enough to react to. The rest you block on a guess. Watch the recovery: d+3, d+4, db+4 and db+3,4 leave her standing, while the FC lows recover crouching, so punish those with a WS launcher. LIB.d+4 at −31 is the biggest punish in the game plan. db+4 and SS.4 crush highs (Wavu cs6~20 / cs6~), so a high jab whiffs into them.

Stance Counterplay↑ Return to Top

LIB — Libertador

What it does (Wavu counterplay): "Libertador Stance (LIB) will automatically evade highs, lows, and throws, but is still vulnerable to mid attacks. Parry window starts very late if Azucena goes into LIB manually (frame 18 onwards)." In LIB she can also press P to parry: LIB.P (High) evades frames 1~16, and LIB.P (Low) is a mid, mid counter at i7~8 (+8 on hit). In Heat the low parry launches and the high parry knocks down.
Entry: manual f+3+4 (evasion from frame 18). From attacks, the plus ones on block: H.2+3 Heat Smash (+6), uf+3+4 (+4~+6), ws3 (+4), LIB.1 (+3), f,f,F+3,2~F (+3), d+2,3 (+1). On hit: LIB.4 (+10), LIB.1 (+8), ws4,1~F (+8), f+3 (+5), BT.3 (+4), b+1,1 (+3). Minus entries: 3,2~F −18, df+1,4,1~F −17, d+4,1~F −20, BT.1,2~F −19, d+1+2 (−4 on block).
Can she block? No source says so. Wavu: LIB is "still vulnerable to mid attacks". She can cancel out with DB (r20).
Lows: LIB.3 (i16~17, −9, into BT) and LIB.d+4 (i19~20, −31). One is −9, the other a launch punish; stand-blocking is the default.
Fastest move: LIB.1 at i13 (high, +3). Fastest mid: LIB.2 at i14~15 (Heat Engager, −8).
Power crush: LIB.3+4 (high, i16~17, −2, homing, power crush 7~15). It absorbs a mid check, so a mid check into LIB is not free — but it's a high, so a crouching mid or a duck beats it.

Heat↑ Return to Top

Heat Smash H.2+3: Mid, Mid, Throw · i15~16 · +6 on block, into LIB. No punish: don't press after blocking it. Wavu: "Hit level becomes m,h,m on block/whiff", "Enter LIB +6 automatically on block/whiff", and it's a Reversal Break (7 chip on block). The anti-strat page: "i15 mid, high, mid, mid. Ends +6 in LIB." After it, you're −6 against a stance that evades highs and lows (see Stance Check).
Heat Burst 2+3: mid, i16, +1 on block, power crush 7~16.

Heat Engagers

3,3 (mid, high; −1) · f+4,4 (mid, high; −7) · LIB.2 (mid, i14~15, −8) · b+3 (mid, i19, +2c, spike) · d+1+3 (crouch throw, i12~13, unbreakable). Two of the five end in a high: ducking the second hit of 3,3 or f+4,4 denies the activation. None is punishable on block. The anti-strat page lists f1+2 as an engager instead of 3,3; Wavu doesn't tag f+1+2, so the guide uses Wavu's list.

Heat-only moves and what Heat changes

Wavu: in Heat her b+2, b+1,1,2 and LIB.1,2 can go ~F into LIB at +4 (210F of Heat each). H.LIB.d+3+4 is a new mid tornado from LIB. H.f+3+4 parries all highs and lows (120F). General Counter Play↑ Return to Top

Practice priorities

1. Check LIB with a mid. It evades highs, lows and throws (Wavu). Her LIB power crush is a high, so a crouching mid is the safest check.
2. SSR her jab and 4-strings; block f+3 (it tracks your SSR) and df+1.
3. Launch the big ones: LIB.d+4 (−31), db+1+2 (−21), FC.d+3 (−17), db+3+4 (−16 up close), FC.d+4 / FC.df+4 (−15).
4. Duck her highs: f+1+2 and LIB.3+4 (high power crushes), b+2 (+2, homing), the 3,3 and f+4,4 Heat Engager enders, 1,2, b+1,1,2.
5. Respect her plus moves: Heat Smash +6, uf+3+4 +4~+6, ws3 +4 and d+1 +4 on block.

Lab drill — training wheels: have the dummy throw the standalone opener, then step. Stage 1 (direction only, timing untested): the dummy plays a lone 4 on fixed timing, and you block, then sidestep right (Wavu: 4,3 whiffs vs SSR, SWR from 1st block; 4 itself tracks SSL, so right is the only side). 20-rep sets, graduate at 18/20 on 3 straight sets (about 60 reps minimum). Stage 2: the dummy plays the real 4,3 with randomized timing (Wavu: can be delayed 12f), 16/20 on 3 straight sets. Stage 3: random playback of 4 alone (−8, take your turn), 4,3 (SSR, or block and punish at −11), 4,1 (high: duck it) and a throw. 25-rep sets, 75% correct decisions on 2 straight sets. Stage 4: log what holds in real matches as Lab (Mike). Why Stage 2 matters for her: the 3 can be delayed by 12 frames, so a step committed too early gets hit. Weaknesses: a standalone opener has no follow-up on a clock, recovers differently from the same hit inside a string, and knowing it's coming removes the read. Rep counts are a heuristic. Cap a session at around 100–150 reps, stop if accuracy falls 15 points, and go back a stage if a stage drops under 60% for two sets.
