How to Run It↑ Return to Top

Short on time? Do the five marked Do first. R1, R2 and R3 decide your game-one habits against Reina (she has taken game one in 7 of your 9 sets), K1 sets your default step against Kazuya, and A1 is the frame race your Armor King plan rests on. They take about 25 minutes together.

Settings to turn on once

  • Frame data display on. Half these tests are settled by reading the frame advantage after the hit or block, so keep it on screen the whole session.
  • Recording + playback. Record the dummy's move in a slot, then play it back. Where a test says random playback, fill two or more slots and turn on random slot playback.
  • "Guaranteed?" tests: set the dummy to guard everything after its recorded move. Anything that still lands was guaranteed; anything it blocks wasn't.
  • Heat: keep Heat refilling for both sides on K7 and the Heat half of V1; leave it normal elsewhere.
  • Menu names move between patches, so look for the setting that does the job if the label differs.

What counts as a result

Run each condition 10 times unless the test says otherwise. Confirmed = the predicted outcome on 8 or more of 10 clean reps. Disproved = the opposite outcome on 8 or more. Unclear = anything in between, or you couldn't get a clean rep (note why in the box). A rep where you were late doesn't count, so redo it rather than scoring it. Rep counts are a rule of thumb, not research.
