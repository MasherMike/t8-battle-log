Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR) in neutral. The TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview (community-written) says "Ninja / SSR". Wavu's per-move notes don't name a neutral side.

Exceptions:
• df+1 (Body Blow, i14~15 mid): Wavu — "Pseudo-homing. Can only be sidewalked left at range." SSR doesn't beat it. It's −2~−1 on block, so block it up close, and only sidewalk left when it's thrown from range. Here the sources disagree: the per-move note (Tier 1) beats the anti-strat's SSR, so follow the note for this move.
• Around 0f with him back-turned: step LEFT (SWL). The anti-strat: "SWL beats literally everything except BT b3 / BT3." Both exceptions are highs (BT.b+3 is homing, i18~19; BT.3 is i10), so a duck covers what SWL misses.
• b+2,4: Wavu — "Whiffs vs SS, SWL from 1st block." Either sidestep works, or a sidewalk left.
• The @Mezerdie sidestep chart says SSL (via sidestep.md), the opposite of the anti-strat. This guide follows the anti-strat (it's the higher tier) but the chart's call lines up with the two Tier 1 notes above, which both point left.
• Homing (list below): f+3, ws3+4, SZN.1+2, BT.b+3 and both generic throws. df+1 is pseudo-homing.

So the habit is: SSR his neutral pokes, block df+1 up close and SWL it at range, SWL when he's back-turned at even frames, and duck BT.b+3 / BT.3. Don't step the homing moves.

Standing Block Punishment↑ Return to Top

Block values are from Wavu's Raven movelist and counterplay tables. 176 moves are on both sites and TekkenDocs agrees on every block value. Where Wavu gives a range (e.g. −14~−12), the move goes in the tier of the less minus number, the one that's always true.

The clone rule (Wavu counterplay): "Every clone outside of heat is −13 on block, special mid. Every clone during heat is −3 on block." His Phantom/clone extensions (the weapon projectile hits) are the most common punish in the matchup. See the −13 to −14 tier.

Three traps. (1) Naked vs extension, both ways: f+2 is −12~−11 but f+2,3 is only −9~−8. b+2 is −14~−12 but b+2,2 is −3 and b+2,4 −7~−6. df+2 is −13~−11, df+2,3 −8~−7, df+2,4 −15~−14. df+4,4 is −14~−13 but df+4,4,3 is −6~−3. Confirm where he stopped. (2) Cancels to BT: uf+4,4 and 3,3,4 can cancel to BT with B (Wavu: −16~−15 for the cancel). ws3+4 also cancels to BT with B. (3) Pushback: the anti-strat flags f,F+2 ("massive pushback"), ws3+4, b,B+2 and b+2,2,3.

Low Block Punishment↑ Return to Top

Reactability tag (Mike's reaction rule, using the low's fastest startup frame): Reactable 25f or slower · Mostly reactable 21–24f · Not reactable — guess or condition 20f or faster. Startups are from the Wavu movelist (for a string whose low is a later hit, Wavu's startup for that hit); a low with no stated startup gets no tag.
His big lows mostly come from BT, FC and late in strings. From neutral, db+3 (i28) is reactable but only −12~−11, and d+4 / db+4 (−13 / −14~−13) are mostly reactable. The launch-size ones are b+2,2,1+2 (−30, not reactable — the anti-strat's b+2 mix), BT.d+3 (−26, reactable), FC.df+3+4 (−23) and u+3,3 (−16, reactable). Many of his lows crouch early, so a high jab whiffs: d+4, db+4 and 2,3 from 6, qcf+2 from 10, BT.d+2/BT.d+3 from 4, and every FC move from 1. db+3 is airborne 8~24.

Duck and Launch↑ Return to Top

The clone enders aren't duckable. Every Phantom/clone hit is a weapon projectile and a special mid (Wavu), so ducking the hit before it doesn't help with the clone. Block it and punish (−13). The anti-strat's duckable-high list: 2,4 · 3,3,4 · 4~3,3 · df+2,3 (mix) · b+2,4 (mix) · BT.1,4.

BT — back turned

• Entry: b+3+4, and many moves recover BT. Plus entries (Wavu): on block u+3 +2, BT.b+3 +4~+5, f,f,F+3 +4~+6, b+4,B+4 +1c~+6c; on hit 2,4~B +8, 4,1 +7~+8, b+4,4 +6c~+8c, db+2, b+2,2, f+3~B +5~+6, and others (see Stance Check).
• Can he block in it? Wavu: BT.b+3+4 (turning to face you) "Can block mid or high attacks after 5f". The anti-strat: "Manual BT transition (b3+4) vulnerable for 5f before blocking." No source says he can block lows in BT.
• Lows: BT.d+1 (special low, −5), BT.d+4 (i12, −15), BT.f+4 (i16, −9), BT.d+2 (i20, −12), BT.f,F+3+4 (i23, −18~−11), BT.d+3 (i25, −26~−24, reactable).
• Parry: BT.f+1+2 (Sixth Sense) parries mid or high punches or kicks 1~8, then a throw follow-up.
• Fastest move: BT.1, a high at i8. BT.2 and BT.3 are i10 highs. All three are highs: a crouch beats them.
• Power crush: BT.1+2 (mid, 6~14, −13). Jab-checking him in BT can get absorbed.
• The answer (anti-strat): around 0f, SWL beats everything except BT.b+3 and BT.3, which are both highs.
Theory (Ledger): since both of the anti-strat's SWL exceptions are highs, mixing SWL with a crouch at even frames covers everything the anti-strat names. The crouch would still lose to his BT mids (BT.f+3, BT.b+2, BT.4), which are −1 to −9, so the cost is damage, not a big punish. Untested.

CD — Shadow Sprint

Entered with qcf, from SZN.F, and with ~F after SZN.1 (+5~+6 on block), BT.2,1 (+3 on block, +7 on hit) and 2,4 (+10 on hit). Wavu lists ub+1, ub+1,2, u+3 and its strings, uf+3, uf+4 / uf+4,4 and uf+3+4 strings as available during CD. He can cancel CD to FC with d,DF.

Blind Roll — the phase-through

3~4,F, BT.3,4,4,F, BT.f+3+4,F and BT.f,f,f: Wavu "Will phase through opponent up-close" and "While vanished, Raven has no hurtbox". The roll versions are −36 to −39 on block. The anti-strat for 3~4: "Mixup if phase or not. Backdash into a move that realigns, or hold slightly forward → BT hopkick to punish the phase."

FC — full crouch

His crouch moves are crouching from frame 1 (Wavu), so a high jab whiffs over them. Plus entries on hit: 2,4~D +8, SZN.3~D and BT.b+2,4~D +5, BT.d+2~D +4. Lows: FC.1 / FC.2 (special lows), FC.3 (−17), FC.4 (−15), FC.df+3 (−9~−7), FC.df+3+4 (−23~−21).

Heat↑ Return to Top

Heat Smash H.2+3: mid → attack throw, i18~20, +14g on block into SZN. No punish. Don't press. The anti-strat: "i18 mid, mid, mid. Ends +14 in SLZ." 9 chip damage on block. From +14, every SZN move except the slow SZN.3+4 beats your jab (see Stance Check).
Heat Burst 2+3: mid, i16, power crush 7~16, +1 on block.
Heat power (anti-strat): hitting the Doppelganger (clone) moves restores Heat. In Heat his clones are −3, not −13 (Wavu counterplay). Don't punish clone enders during Heat.
Theory (Ledger): the clone extensions are "available only on hit or block", and in Heat they're −3 and restore Heat when they hit. So blocking the first hit and then the clone shouldn't cost you anything in Heat, but it's no longer your turn. Expect to defend, not punish, until his Heat runs out. Untested.

Stance Check — can a jab interrupt his plus stance entries?↑ Return to Top

Arithmetic only, all from Wavu. Effective startup = his raw startup − his frame advantage. Your jab is 10f, so effective i9 or faster → jab can't interrupt; exactly i10 → trades; i11 or slower → a 10f jab interrupts. Where Wavu gives a startup range, the fastest frame is used; where it gives an advantage range (e.g. +7c~+8c), the larger number is used, the worst case for you. Only entries with a stated plus advantage are covered; airborne (a) and knockdown (d) advantages and forced tech rolls are left out.
Mike's rule: a character can't block in a unique stance unless a source says otherwise. No source says Raven can block in SZN or CD. For BT, Wavu says turning to face you (BT.b+3+4) "can block mid or high attacks after 5f"; FC is his ordinary full crouch, not a unique stance.
State changes the read (Wavu/TekkenDocs): a standing jab is a high, so it whiffs against crouching moves (SZN.4 from 6, BT.d+1/BT.d+4 from 1, BT.d+2/BT.d+3/BT.f,F+3 from 4, every FC move from 1). BT.4 (6~19), BT.f+3+4 (5~24), BT.3+4 (13~31), SZN.3 (9~17) and the CD moves are airborne early; that beats lows, not a high jab. Homing: SZN.1+2, BT.b+3.
