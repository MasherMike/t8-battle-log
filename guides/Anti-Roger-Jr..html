New character, thin sources. Roger Jr. arrived in 3.03.01. As of 2026-11-27 Wavu has frame data for most of his moves (startup on 117 of 138 rows, block on 127), but TekkenDocs has no frame-data or anti-strat page yet, so nothing here is cross-checked and there is no community step advice. Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: lean Sidestep RIGHT (SSR). Thin evidence. No Tier 2 source exists for Roger Jr.: TekkenDocs has no anti-strat page and he isn't on the sidestep chart (sidestep.md). Tier 1 (Wavu per-move notes) has one step note: df+2 Bandicoot Bomb, "Good sidestep left tracking". It's a mid, i15, −7, and goes +9 into Rocket Stance on hit (+18 on counter hit), so it's one of his main ROC entries. That one note says SSL is the risky side. Nothing in the data says what SSR beats.

Exceptions:
• Homing (Wavu), don't step: df+3 (mid, −8), uf+1 (high, −7), uf+3 (mid, high, −9), AST.4 (high, +4) and both standard throws 1+3 / 2+4.
• Two of the homing attacks are highs (uf+1, AST.4): duck those instead of stepping.

So the habit is: don't SSL into df+2; if you step, step right; duck the homing highs; block the homing mids.

General Counter Play↑ Return to Top

Practice priorities

1. Launch the big ones. 3+4 Tail Launcher −20, the db+4 kick chain −19 at every step (−17 at the end), db+3 −27, ws1 and df+1+4 −16.
2. Respect Astronauts. Heat Smash and b+3+4 leave him plus in AST (+13 / +5). At +13 you can't press. Block the mids, duck AST.4.
3. Duck his highs. f+2 (+3, chip), the d+1+2 tree, the 1,2,x enders, H.f+2 strings, BOB.1 (unblockable).
4. Don't SSL into df+2. It tracks left and puts him +9 in ROC on hit.
5. Punish the −13 Heat Engagers b+2,4 and ROC.4,3.
6. Learn which lows are worth guessing. Only b+4 is reactable, and it's −10. db+3 (−27) and d+4 (−15) are the big ones.

Evasion to know

Crouching (cs) attacks that duck your highs: d+1, d+2, d+3, d+4, db+4, f+3 (a high that crouches), b+2,4 (2nd hit), SS.4, hFC.1 / hFC.2 (cs1~34). Theory (Ledger): Because f+2 (high, i13) is +3 on block with chip, and d+1+2 opens an all-high tree, a crouch is his biggest weakness on paper.
