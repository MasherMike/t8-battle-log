Game-one rules vs Reina

1. Empty SEN after df+1 / f+2,3 / b+1,1,3: jab it, or duck if you read SEN.3. Respect f,F+2 (+2 into SEN).
2. Block low, launch with ws1. Her −17 standing strings (1,1,2 and ws4,4) are df+2.
3. In her Heat, stop pressing mids. Heat Smash is +8, 3,4,H.4 is only −8, and H.db+1+2 auto-parries mids and highs. Throw or low her while she holds WRA, and punish only once she's clearly stopped.

Rep Rules↑ Return to Top

These are the training-wheel rules from your guides, applied the same way to every drill here.
Stage 1 (fixed timing, one move): 20-rep sets, graduate at 18/20 on 3 straight sets. My reading: the three sets can run across sessions, so a drill with 2 sets today can graduate on its next day.
Stage 2 (random playback): 16/20 on 3 straight sets.
Stage 3 (full mix, read included): 25-rep sets, 75% correct on 2 straight sets.
Stage 4: log what holds up in real matches.
Stop rules: cap a session around 100–150 reps; stop if accuracy drops 15 points from where you started; go back a stage if you're under 60% for two sets.
Rep counts are a rule of thumb, not research. One pass mark per week is enough: a drill that clears its mark three weeks running can drop to the warm-up or the Day 5 gauntlet.
