Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR). All three sources point the same way. Wavu's own notes have three of his strings whiffing on SSR, the TekkenDocs anti-strat page lists his most-used pokes (df+2, 1,2, b+1, uf+4, uf+3) as "mostly SSR", and the @Mezerdie sidestep chart says SSR for the whole character. Tally: 9 moves lose to SSR, 5 to a left step.

Exceptions — these are the ones that catch the SSR habit:
• df+1 is his main fast mid (i13, −1) and Wavu says it "tracks SSR/SWR completely". The anti-strat page says sidewalk LEFT. Don't step it right; it's −1, so just block it.
• f+3 (i20, goes into SNK) has "good right sidestep tracking" (Wavu); step it left.
• b+3+4 (his high power crush), d+4 and SS.2 lose to a left step (anti-strat). b+3+4 is a high, so duck it instead.
• f+4, df+3 and both standard throws are homing. Stepping does nothing; see the list below.
• The anti-strat page says you often have to keep sidewalking, not single-step, to get past his extensions.

Duck and Launch↑ Return to Top

SNK — Stealth Step

Raw entry is df+3+4. Plus entries (his turn): SNK.1+2 +4c, Heat H.f+1+2 +4c, f+3+4 +3c. Minus entries with DF on block: 3, 2,3 or 1,2,3 −1 · df+4,1 −2 · db+2 −3 · f+3 −3 · ws3 −7 · d+3 −10 · f+2,4 −11 · FC.df+4 −11 · 1,2,2 −13. (Wavu, with the anti-strat page agreeing.)

Can he block in SNK? Neither Wavu nor TekkenDocs says. Lows: only SNK.4 (i20~22, −14), plus the Heat Smash in Heat (also −14). Both slow and punishable, so stand-block is the default. Fastest move: SNK.1, a high at i12. Power crush or parry from SNK: none listed.

How to deal with it: after his −1 to −3 entries, he's in SNK with one high (i12) and slow mids and lows. Theory (Ledger): after a −3 entry (db+2 or f+3 into SNK), your i10 jab arrives on your frame 10, before his fastest option SNK.1 can land (his frame 12 + 3 = your frame 15). That only holds if SNK is a standing state that a jab can hit; nothing says whether SNK crouches, so check it in the lab before relying on it. Against −1 entries the margin is one frame; just block. After a plus entry (SNK.1+2, f+3+4) it's his turn: stand-block, duck if you see Al-Ghul coming.

Both standard throws take either break, so on a read, press 1+2 or just 1 and you're covered for both. The only grab that needs a specific break is uf+1+2 (break 1+2). He has no command throw that needs its own break; his throws-on-hit (b+2, f,b+2, SNK.df+1,2, the Heat Smash) all start as attacks you block or duck.

His power crush is a HIGH — duck it. If you've been playing characters whose armor moves are mids, this habit is the wrong way round here: crouch and launch b+3+4.
