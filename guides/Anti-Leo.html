Leo on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR). Wavu's per-move notes give two strings that whiff to the right (d+4,2, uf+2,1), one that whiffs to the left (b+1,4) and two that whiff either way. The TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview (community-written) says the same thing in three words: "Weak to SSR." The @Mezerdie sidestep chart (via sidestep.md) also says SSR. All three agree.

Exceptions:
• b+1,4 (the +9 knee into KNK) whiffs to the LEFT from 1st block — but its sister b+1,1+2 tracks (anti-strat), and it's -12, so SSL after b+1 is a read, not a habit.
• uf+1,1 / uf+1,2: the anti-strat page says both can be SSR'd only if he delays; undelayed they track.
• After CD.1 hits you (he's -1), the anti-strat page says SWL beats every follow-up except ws1+2 and dash/delayed moves.
• Five things are homing (list below), including the plus-on-block high BOK.3 — duck that one, don't step it.
• Leo's own f+2+3 sidesteps to the right (Wavu) — it moves, it doesn't track you.

So the habit is: SSR in neutral and against his standing strings; block the b+1 family and punish the -12.

Standing Block Punishment

Watch the stance escapes. Many of his punishable strings can stop early or cancel into KNK, BOK or CD. The cancel usually keeps him minus — and BOK and CD cannot block (Wavu), so a minus entry into them is still yours. The real trap is the +9 knee: b+1 (-12) and b+4,1 (-12) both have a knee follow-up (b+1,4 / b+4,1~4) that is +9 into KNK. Confirm the string ended before you punish.

Stance Check — can a jab interrupt his plus stance entries?

Arithmetic only, all from Wavu. Effective startup = his raw startup − his frame advantage. Your jab is 10f, so effective i9 or faster → jab can't interrupt; exactly i10 → trades; i11 or slower → a 10f jab interrupts. Where Wavu gives a range, the fastest frame is used. Only entries with a stated plus advantage on block are covered.
Mike's rule: a character can't block in a unique stance unless a source says otherwise. Here: Wavu says BOK and CD cannot block; the Wavu counterplay page says KNK can cancel to block (and to a low parry).
State changes the read: a standing jab is a high, so it whiffs against crouching moves — every BOK move but BOK.3 and BOK.4 crouches from frame 1, and CD is crouching 1~20. BOK.1+2 is a power crush (armor 8~19). No KNK or CD move is a power crush. Where the Wavu counterplay page's KNK percentages disagree with the arithmetic, that is noted in the row.
