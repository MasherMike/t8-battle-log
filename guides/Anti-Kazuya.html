Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep / Sidewalk LEFT (SSL / SWL). Every source agrees, which is rare. Wavu's counterplay page says "Majority of his moves are weak to sidestep / sidewalk left. For example his hellsweep." The TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview says "SSL / SWL", and the @Mezerdie sidestep chart says SSL. Wavu's per-move notes back it up: every string with a "Whiffs vs" note loses to SSL.

Exceptions — these catch the SSL habit:
• b+2 (i14~15 mid, −8) tracks SSL; the TekkenDocs Kazuya guide (written for Kazuya players) says SSR is what beats it. It's his main mid check for exactly this reason.
• In Heat, DVK.f,F+2 has "increased realign tracking to left sidesteps" (Wavu). Don't SSL Devil Fist while he's in Devil form.
• His Heat Smash H.2+3 "tracks both sides" (Wavu). Crouch-block it instead; see Heat.
• Five moves and both generic throws are homing; stepping does nothing. Three of them are highs, so duck those.

Stance Counterplay↑ Return to Top

Wind God Step / crouch dash (the wavedash)

Entered with f,n,d,DF; 3,1 also transitions to it with df (+2 on block). It recovers in full crouch at frame 20, he can cancel it into block with b on frames 1~6 and into a sidestep with u (Wavu). Chaining it is the wavedash.
Can he block in it? Only by cancelling with b in frames 1~6 — so a wavedash that keeps going isn't blocking.
Its one low: the hellsweep cd4, i16, −23 on block. Everything else from it is mid or high, so stand-block is the default and you react low.
Fastest move out: EWGF, "actual startup i14 (i13 if done from Mist Step)" (Wavu), +5 on block.
Power crush from it: none outside Heat.
Theory (Ledger): his wavedash is crouching, so a jab thrown into it likely whiffs and gets EWGF'd. Check a wavedash with a quick mid, not a jab. Still to lab test.

Heat↑ Return to Top

Heat Smash H.2+3: low first, i18, −14 on block, tracks both sides, Balcony Break. Don't step it and don't stand-block it — crouch-block the low and take your crouching −13/−14 punish (ws1+2 or uf+1,1). Wavu calls it "a fast unseeable low", so it's a guess, but it's a guess where blocking pays.

Takeaway: two of his five Heat Engagers are highs at the end (df+1,2 and b+4). Ducking those denies his Heat, not just damage.

Okizeme — when you're knocked down

Wavu: "Kazuya has incredible okizeme." His safe options on a grounded opponent are the mids f,F+3 and f,F+2; the riskier ones are the lows d+1+2 and cd4,1. Wavu's key advice: "you can just keep laying down." If you stay down, he'll likely use f,F+4, which you can sidestep-roll and which does much less damage.
