Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR) once you've blocked the opener of a string. Wavu's per-move notes have 5 of her strings whiffing on SSR/SWR and only one on SSL. That includes her bread-and-butter jab string 1,1,2 (from 2nd block), 3,4 (from 1st block) and the matching stance strings SET.1,2,2, BT.1,2,1 and KAT.3,2. Four more strings (1,1,4, BT.1,2,4, SET.1,2,3, SET.1,2,4) whiff on a step to either side. The @Mezerdie sidestep chart also says SSR.

Exceptions — these are the ones that catch the SSR habit:
• KAT.4,2 (low, high) whiffs on SSL from 1st block, not SSR. You're crouch-blocking the low anyway, so staying down also beats the high.
• 3 (Crescent Kick) and BT.4 are homing highs — duck them, don't step. Both standard throws are homing too.
• KAT.1 "has good left sidestep tracking" (Wavu). It's another reason to step right, not left, around Katon.
• The kunai moves (qcf+1, d+3,4,1) are special mids; Wavu has no step note for them. Block them (−13).

Standing Block Punishment↑ Return to Top

Block values are from Wavu's Kunimitsu counterplay tables and movelist; all 167 moves on both sites match TekkenDocs. For your Victor answer to each bucket, use the ★ rows of your Victor Punish Sheet: −10/−11 1,2 · −12 2,2,2 · −13 uf+1,1 · −14 f+4,1 · −15/−16 df+2 · −17+ b+3,1.

Most of her strings stop at −9 or better. The jab strings (1,1,2, 2,2,2, BT.1,2,2, SET.1,2,2, KAT.1,2) are all −9, so the punishes come from her launchers and big commitments: f,F+4,2 (−17~−16), b+3,4 (−20), b+4,3 (−16), ub+3+4 (−17) and the kunai throw at −13. Stance Counterplay↑ Return to Top

Four stances, one rule: stand-block by default. Katon (KAT) and Setsunagake (SET) each have one low (KAT.4, SET.3), BT has two (BT.d+2, BT.d+4), and every one of those lows is −10 or worse. The stances she gets plus into are SET (f+4~F +3 and BT.4~F +6 on block) and BT/KAT on hit. The arithmetic for when your jab gets in is in Stance Check.

Can she block in KAT? No source says she can. Low: KAT.4 (i22~28, −16~−10); KAT.4,2 adds a high. Heat adds H.KAT.d+1+2 (low, −14).
