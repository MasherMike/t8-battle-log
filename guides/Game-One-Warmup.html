Reina Rules · Game One↑ Return to Top

Trigger: the first time Reina shows up in a session. Your first-Reina-of-the-session game ones are 1–6. When she's your second or third Reina of the night, game one goes 2–1.
1. Empty SEN: after df+1 into SEN she can't block until frame 15. Jab it, or duck if you read SEN.3 (high, homing, +1 on block).
2. Block low, launch: d+4 is −15 and db+2 is −16. Both are .
3. In her Heat, stop pressing mids. Throw or low her while she holds WRA, and punish only once she's clearly stopped.
Rules 1–2 match Wavu today. Rule 3 is from your Anti-Reina guide, which has the frame details.
