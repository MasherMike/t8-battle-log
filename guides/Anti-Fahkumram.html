Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR). Wavu's own notes say his charged knee and tornado follow-ups whiff a right step or walk from the first block (3,4~3, 1,2,4,3~4, f+2,1,GRF.2), and the @Mezerdie sidestep chart gives SSR for his whole character too, so the two tiers agree.

Exceptions — these catch the SSR habit:
• 3+4 (−9, crumple on counter hit) and df+3+4 (−10) both have "good right sidestep tracking" per Wavu. Block them.
• b+3, f,F+4, ws2,4, 3,4~4 and the two "Maw" feints are homing; stepping does nothing. b+3 and 3,4~4's second hit are highs, so duck those.
• b,f+4 (+6, crumple) tracks left, so it's fine against your SSR, but don't drift left against it.
• His own db+3+4 sidesteps to the right as it attacks.

Stance Counterplay↑ Return to Top

GRF — Garuda Force (a power-up, not a stance)

How he gets it: on hit with df+1,2, f+3+4, b+4,3,2, f,F+1+2, RAM.1+3, the counter-hit low attack throws (d+4, 1,2,4,4, 3,1,d+4), and a successful b+2+4 punch parry. He can also use GRF moves any time he's in Heat. A GRF move uses it up (and burns Heat time).

What it powers up: his charged knee and charged tornado become Guard Breaks that are +13 on block and drop him into RAM, and they can't be reversed. GRF.1+2 becomes a power crush that only attacks after it absorbs a hit.

The answer: every charged move is i32+ effective and Wavu says he can't block during the charge, so any attack from the block before beats it (interrupt with i32 from the 3rd block of 1,2,4, i34 from the 1st block of 3,4). Or step: the charged versions whiff a sidestep or sidewalk either way. Watch for the feints: a cancel to crouch, or a back-input "Maw" that is homing and −7.

Entered at +13 for him from a blocked GRF charged knee or tornado, from GRF.1+2 after it absorbs, or from df+3+4,GRF.3 on hit. Wavu's stance notes: moves can be delayed by 75F, and he goes back to blocking (i23) with b or db or after 197F. Wavu also writes "Cannot block i10 moves" under the stance; Theory (Ledger): that reads as "he can't block in RAM until he leaves it", so if you get a turn, take it. Untested.

His options: one mid that's +1, one mid at −11, a low at −13, an unblockable, and an unbreakable throw that hits high.

Parries and unblockables

Two parries, and they split by limb. b+1+3 parries high, mid and low kicks (frames 5~12) and turns into an attack throw. b+2+4 parries high and mid punches (frames 5~12), gives him GRF and 10 recoverable health. If he's fishing, check with the other limb, a low punch, or a throw.
Unblockables: b+1+4 (i60) and RAM.2 (i40). Step or hit him first.
