Movement — Which Way to Step↑ Return to Top

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR) once you've blocked the opener of a string. Wavu's per-move notes have 11 of his strings whiffing on SSR/SWR and only one (df+1,1) whiffing on a left step. That covers his bread-and-butter strings: 1,2, 2,1,2, f+1,1, df+1,2, b+1,2, b+3,2 and b+3,2,2. The TekkenDocs anti-strat page adds df+2, 2, db+4 and f+2 as SSR.

Exceptions — these are the ones that catch the SSR habit:
• His raw pokes are split. The anti-strat page says SSL for df+1, db+1 and 3 (f+3). So the SSR rule is for strings after the first block, not for every raw poke.
• df+1,1 whiffs on SSL/SWL (Wavu), while df+1,2 whiffs on SSR/SWR. The anti-strat page says you can SSL both df+1 branches; Wavu disagrees on df+1,2, and this guide follows Wavu. Don't guess the step here: block df+1 (−4) and play from there.
• f+1 and df+4 "seem homing" (anti-strat); Wavu calls f+1 "effectively homing?". Don't step them.
• Against Gamma Howl (GMH) the anti-strat page says "a big SSL or small SWL", and that GMH.4 "will most likely switch sides if you step".
• Both standard throws, 1+2, ub+1+2, f,F+1+2, the Debugger low and GMH.1 are homing; see the list below.
• The @Mezerdie sidestep chart says SSL for the whole character. It disagrees with Wavu's string notes; this guide follows Wavu (Tier 1) for strings and keeps SSL for the raw pokes and GMH where the anti-strat page says so.

Evidence tier: the default comes from Tier 1, Wavu's per-move whiff notes (TekkenDocs' frame table carries the same notes): 11 strings lose to SSR, 1 to SSL, 5 to either side. Tier 2 is split: the TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview (community-written) gives four raw moves to SSR and three to SSL, plus SSL against GMH, and the @Mezerdie sidestep chart says SSL overall. The tiers disagree, so this guide follows Tier 1 for strings after the first block and keeps the anti-strat SSL calls for the raw pokes and GMH. Weighted by use: his jab and mid-poke strings (1,2, 2,1,2, df+1,2, b+1,2, b+3,2) are what he throws most, and they all lose to SSR. Nothing here is lab-tested yet. The anti-strat page also notes: "Can't move well."

Low Block Punishment↑ Return to Top

Reactability tag (Mike's reaction rule, using the low's fastest startup frame): Reactable 25f or slower · Mostly reactable 21–24f · Not reactable — guess or condition 20f or faster. Startups are from the Wavu movelist (for a string whose low is a later hit, Wavu's startup for that hit); a low with no stated startup gets no tag.
After a blocked low you're crouching, so use the crouching rows of your Victor Punish Sheet: −11/−12 ws4 · −13/−14 ws1+2 or uf+1,1 · −15+ ws1 launch.

His fast lows are mostly −11 to −15; his slow ones are launch-punishable. db+2 (i22, −18), db+3 (i21, −20), d+3 (i20, −17) and the Debugger (i29, −37) are the big ones. Stance Counterplay↑ Return to Top

GMH — Gamma Howl

Raw entry is 3+4 (also ws3+4): an armor stance with power crush frames 7~22. If it absorbs a hit he gains Gamma Charge (GMC) and heals recoverable health. Plus entries (his turn): SS.2~D +9 · Heat H.f+3+4~D +8 · Make Some Noise (2,1,uf+1+2, FC.df+3+4,uf+1+2, GMH.uf+1+2) +5 · 1,1~D and f,F+1~D +4 · 3~D +2. Even or minus: b+3,2~D +0 · f+3+4~D −3. uf+3 goes into GMH automatically on hit (+8c). (Wavu; the anti-strat list agrees except df+2,1, which it gives as −37; Wavu −32.)

Can he block in GMH? Neither Wavu nor TekkenDocs says. Lows: none — no GMH move is a low. What punishes you for crouching is two unbreakable crouch throws, GMH.1+3 and GMH.2+4 (i15). So the default is stand-block, and duck only on a read of GMH.2 / GMH.4 (both highs). Fastest move: GMH.4, a high at i14 (anti-strat agrees: "fastest high i14"). Fastest mid is GMH.f+2 at i17 (the anti-strat page says i22). Power crush: GMH.3+4, armor from frame 6 — so a jab check into GMH can get absorbed. Anti-strat: "You can jab and still block shockwave from a +4 transition. For worse transitions you can 1,2 / df1 / ... and still block."

Gamma Charge (GMC) — a buff, not a stance

He gets GMC by absorbing a hit with 3+4, or on hit/block of the charged moves (b+2*, f+1,2,2*, b+3,2,2*, db+2*, f,F+2*, FC.df+3+4*, FC.db+1,1,1*), and it is always on in Heat. It upgrades three GMH moves: GMC.1 (homing mid, −6, launches), GMC.2 (high, power crush from frame 1, +11 guard break) and GMC.3+4 (special mid, −9). Watch for the blue glow after a charged move connects.
