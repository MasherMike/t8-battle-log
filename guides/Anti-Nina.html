Nina on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR), up close. Of the Wavu per-move whiff notes that pick a side, eight pick the right and three the left: 4,3, df+3,1, df+3,3, db+4,3, b+1,4, f,f,F+3,3 and ws4,3 whiff vs SSR from 1st block, ws4,2 vs SSR from 1st hit. The TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview (community-written) agrees: "SSR is strong close, at -3 (f3) it avoids standard stuff like jabs, df1,2, df4, df3, df2, m4, b1+2, db3…", and the @Mezerdie chart (via sidestep.md) also says SSR.

Exceptions:
• df+1,2,1 whiffs vs SSL from 1st block, and ws1,4 vs SWL from 1st block (Wavu). 4,4,3+4 only whiffs a delayed SWL from 2nd block ("must perform SSL→SWL").
• CD.2 (qcf+2, a Heat Engager): anti-strat — "QCF2 tracks SSR". Wavu has no tracking note for it.
• Homing — don't step: 1+2 (the anti-strat's "i16 mid homing"), 3+4, b+2, db+2, the 2nd hit of f+3, and both generic throws (list below). The anti-strat adds "d2 almost homing".
• b+3+4 (Out of Sight, high) "has good left sidestep tracking" (Wavu) — it catches SSL, so it's another reason to go right; it's high, so ducking is cleaner.
• 4,3: anti-strat — "4,3 can SS both ways"; Wavu says SSR.

So the habit is: block, then SSR vs her pokes and 2-hit strings up close; SSL vs df+1,2,1; don't step the homing list or CD.2.

Standing Block Punishment

Three traps. (1) Cancels: many of her strings can cancel into Sidestep (SS, D or U), Ducking Step (CD, F) or Sway (SWA, B). Most cancels from punishable moves are still minus, but a few turn a minus move plus: SS.1 is -10, yet SS.1~F / SS.1~B is +10g; 3+4 is -10 but 3+4~F is -6. Confirm she didn't cancel before you press. (2) Naked vs extension: df+3 is -11, but df+3,2 is +1 and df+3,3 -10; 3 is -16 but 3,3,3,4 is -6; 1,2 is -3 while 1,2,1+2 is -14. (3) Pushback: the anti-strat flags u+3 / ub+3 (-15) as pushback punishes and "hard to differentiate from uf3" (-10).

Low Block Punishment

Reactability tag (Mike's reaction rule, using the low's fastest startup frame): Reactable 25f or slower · Mostly reactable 21–24f · Not reactable — guess or condition 20f or faster. Startups are from the Wavu movelist (for a string's low hit, Wavu's startup for that hit); a low with no stated startup gets no tag.
Nina's lows are fast and almost all unreactable — but the everyday ones are punishable: d+4 (i12, -15), d+3 (i16, -17), db+3 (i20, -13), d+2 (i20, -12). Her crouch lows recover crouching, so use WS punishes. The two you can see coming are df+3+4 (i31~35, -37) and the low 3rd hit of 4,4,4 (i30~31). The low that isn't punishable is db+4 (Slicer, -9a, knocks you down), and d+4,1 is +0. Wavu counterplay "Some Lows In Strings": 4,4,4 and uf+4,3,4.

Duck and Launch

Nina's strings end in highs, and the gunshot enders are her chip-damage engine. Wavu counterplay: "Both of these strings end in gunshots. They are safe on block, have some pushback, and do a ridiculous amount of chip. Don't let her get away with these!" Duck 1,2,1,1+2, df+1,2,1,1+2, ws1,1+2, ws4,3,1+2, CD.4,3,1+2 and b+3+4,1+2 — every gunshot hit is high (all -9 outside Heat, -2 in Heat). Each tree has one mid branch to punish instead: 1,2,1+2 / df+1,2,1+2 are -14. Jails: 1,2 and df+1,2 jail, so in those trees you can only duck the 3rd hit, and the gunshots jail from the hit before them — duck the first high you can, and stay down.
