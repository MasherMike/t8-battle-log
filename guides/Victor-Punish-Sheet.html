How to read it: find how minus his move was, use the ★ row. Yours = already in your punish table (repo notes). New = an upgrade from Wavu's Victor punisher page. Damage is the guaranteed punish, before any follow-up. "a" = airborne on hit (launch or juggle), "gc" = ground crumple, "c" = he recovers crouching.

Practice first — the three that pay the most

  1. -14 → f+4,1: 36 dmg vs 30 for uf+1,1 (+20%), and TekkenDocs says it breaks walls and engages Heat on hit. New to your table.
  2. -12 → 2,2,2: 32 dmg vs 22 for 2,1~F (+45%), with Wall Crush +12g. Use 2,1~F only when you want Perfumer +7.
  3. Crouching -15+ → ws1: launches (+53a) vs 16 for ws4. Every blocked low at -15 or worse is a full combo you're leaving on the table if you default to ws4.
Lab drill — training wheels: record the dummy doing one move from a bucket (e.g. a -14 string), block it, punish with the ★ row. Stage 1: 20 reps, graduate at 18/20 on 3 straight sets. Stage 2: record two moves from different buckets on random playback, so you have to read the frame value, not just react. Cap a session at ~100–150 reps; rep counts are a heuristic, not research-derived.

Low Parry↑ Return to Top

Generic low parry | df timed as the low lands | reward frames not checked
Lab (Mike), 21 Nov 2026: a low parry nets an immediate tornado bound for any character, and you pick up the bound combo: 51 damage. The latest input timing is still unmeasured (V3, unclear).
A community Victor sheet in your Drive lists it as "DF at the good timing" | Wavu's movelist doesn't list it (it's a system move) | Weapon lows say "unparryable" on Wavu: block those and use the crouching table
IAI parry | Parries low attacks (Wavu) | parry frames 3?~11? | crouching 4~17
Wavu confirms it parries lows; the crouching frames are also why TekkenDocs calls it a high crush. Needs IAI first (2,2,2 ends in it; uf+1,1 cancels into it with D or 3+4)
Lab (Mike), 21 Nov 2026: parries lows only, and the combo after it is bigger than a normal low parry: 72 damage (73 with Heat bound) vs 51 (V4).
Theory (Ledger): if you see a slow, predictable low (i18+), the parry pays more than block-then-ws4 at -11/-12. Against lows at -15+, blocking and taking ws1 is already a launch, so the parry adds risk for little gain.
