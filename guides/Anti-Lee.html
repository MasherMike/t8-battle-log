Lee on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR), up close. Every Wavu per-move whiff note that picks a side picks the right: 1,2,2,3 whiffs vs SSR from 3rd block, 2,2,3 vs SSR from 2nd block, 4,3,4 vs SSR/SW from 2nd block, 4,u+3 vs SSR/SW from 2nd block, d+4,n,4 vs SSR from 1st hit (Wavu: "cannot SSR on block"). The TekkenDocs anti-strat Quick overview (community-written) agrees: "I would SSR close… SSR at -1 > df4, df2, d3, m4, jabs, d2".

Exceptions:
• df+1 (i13~14, -1): anti-strat — "df1 seems to track both sides after a jab… You have to SSL df1, but can't after a jab anyway." So SSL is the side vs a naked df+1, and after his jab don't step it at all.
• Heat moves: H.d+1+2 "has good right sidestep tracking" (it catches your SSR); H.df+3+4 "has good left sidestep tracking" (Wavu).
• Homing — don't step: 1+2, b,B+4, HMS.3 and both generic throws (list below).
• b+1,1,2: anti-strat — "SWR in neutral beats it."
• At range the sources split. The Wavu counterplay page says "Neutral: Sidewalk left to avoid Lee's main approach options" and "HMS: SSR against his stance pressure"; the @Mezerdie chart (via sidestep.md) says SSL. Neither names the moves it means, so they don't set the default — but it's a real disagreement: see the evidence note below.

So the habit is: SSR after you block a poke up close and vs Hitman pressure; SSL a naked df+1; don't step 1+2, b,B+4 or HMS.3.

Standing Block Punishment

Three traps. (1) HMS cancels: a lot of his punishable moves can go into Hitman Stance with 3 / 4 / 3+4 — usually at the same block frame (1,2,4 → HMS -14; 4,4,4,4 family; ws3,3 → HMS -15), so the punish stays. Where it changes the frame, it's noted. (2) Magic 4 tree: 4,3 -14 and 4,4 / 4,4,4 -13, but the just-frame 4,4:4 is only -9 and 4,u+3 -1 — see Duck and Launch. (3) Pushback: the anti-strat flags 1,3:3:3 (-17) and ub+3 (-13); f+2,1 is "-10 at wall" and b+1,1,2 "-11 at wall".

Low Block Punishment

Reactability tag (Mike's reaction rule, using the low's fastest startup frame): Reactable 25f or slower · Mostly reactable 21–24f · Not reactable — guess or condition 20f or faster. Startups are from the Wavu movelist (for a string's low hit, Wavu's startup for that hit); a low with no stated startup gets no tag.
Lee's everyday lows are fast and unreactable but punishable: d+4 (i12, -13), db+3 (i20, -12), b+3 (i15, -14), and the counter-hit launcher d+3 (i16, -15, +17a on CH, high-crushing 6~47 — Wavu counterplay calls it a main threat). Most of his crouch lows (db+4, FC.3, FC.df+4, Slide) recover crouching, so use WS punishes. The slow ones you can see coming are 2,1,4 (i25) and MS.3+4 (i25~31).

Duck and Launch

Lee is a high-string character. His Magic 4 tree, his jabs (1,2 / 1,3 / 2,1), f+1+2, b+2,4,3, HMS.1,1,1 and both power crushes (b+3+4, HMS.3) are highs. The mid-high enders (1,2,2,3, 2,2,3, df+4,4, f+2,1) are -3 to -10 — ducking turns them into launches. The ones that jail (df+3,2 undelayed, 3,3 undelayed, 4,4, 1,2,4) can't be ducked after the first block.
